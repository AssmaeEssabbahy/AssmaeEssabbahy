### `INDUSTRIAL ENGINEERING × DATA × INTELLIGENT SYSTEMS`
<p align="center">
  I’m an Industrial & Logistics Engineer driven by a simple question:
  <strong>how can we make an industrial system perform better?</strong>
<p align="center">
  I like getting close to the system, breaking complex problems down, understanding
  what really drives performance, and turning operational challenges into
  measurable improvements.
</p>

<p align="center">
  What excites me most is the intersection between industrial engineering,
  data and technology using  data analysis and visualization, AI and digital
  technologies to understand what is happening, uncover patterns, anticipate what
  comes next, and support better decisions.
</p>

  I’m especially drawn to projects where engineering meets intelligence:

### 🚚 Vehicle Routing Optimization — CVRP

> I developed an intelligent decision-support system to solve the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP), with the objective of optimizing delivery routes while respecting vehicle capacity constraints. I implemented two complementary metaheuristics, Genetic Algorithm (GA) and Ant Colony Optimization (ACO), to search for efficient routing solutions. I used Optuna to automate hyperparameter tuning and integrated OSRM to calculate routes based on the real road network. Finally, I developed an interactive Tkinter/TkinterMapView dashboard to compare algorithm performance, visualize convergence and display the optimized routes on a map.

### 📈 AI-Based Industrial Production Forecasting

> I built a Python-based decision-support tool for industrial production forecasting and S&OP. I compared Holt-Winters, ANN, RNN and LSTM models to identify the best approach for predicting future production volumes.
>
> I automated model tuning with Optuna, Keras Tuner and Grid Search, and evaluated performance using RMSE, MAE, MAPE and R². I then integrated the forecasting results into an interactive Tkinter dashboard to support production planning and data-driven decisions.
